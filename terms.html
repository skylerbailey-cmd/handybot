6.1 Recall Alerts (CPSC Data)

The Service retrieves publicly available recall notices from the U.S. Consumer Product Safety Commission (CPSC) and attempts to match them against items in your home inventory using make, model, and product type. Recall data is provided for informational purposes only. Matching is approximate and may produce false positives, false negatives, or delayed results. We do not control CPSC data, we are not affiliated with or endorsed by the CPSC, and we make no guarantee that every relevant recall will be surfaced or surfaced promptly. For the authoritative list of recalls and remedies you should always consult cpsc.gov and the manufacturer of the affected product. If you believe an item in your home has been recalled, stop using it immediately and follow the manufacturer's instructions.

The Insurance PDF export generates a Schedule of Personal Property using replacement-cost values and a straight-line depreciation model (with a 10% floor) based on the age and typical lifespan of each item. Insurers and adjusters determine actual loss settlements under the terms of your policy. You are responsible for reviewing the exported document for accuracy before sharing it with anyone.

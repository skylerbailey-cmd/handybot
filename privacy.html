Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 12, 2026 · Effective date: April 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how grnd-lab ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and shares information about you when you use the HandyBot mobile application and related services (collectively, the "Service"). By using the Service, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide

  • Photos and Images. When you use HandyBot to diagnose repairs, catalog home inventory items, or verify completed work, you upload photos from your device's camera or photo library.
  • Text Content. Messages you send in the Ask HandyBot chat, repair notes, manual repair logs, home names, addresses (optional), and other text you enter into the app.
  • Account Information. If you create an account, we collect your email address and a locally-generated user ID. (Note: the current MVP uses local-only accounts with no server-side authentication.)
  • Subscription Information. If you subscribe to a paid plan, billing is handled by the Apple App Store or Google Play Store. We receive a subscription status but do not collect or store your payment card details.
  • Support Communications. When you contact us at support@grnd-lab.com, we retain the content of your message and any attachments.

1.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • Device Information. Device model, operating system version, app version, language, and time zone.
  • Usage Data. Features you use, actions you take in the app, and crash logs (if enabled).
  • Push Notification Tokens. If you enable reminders, we use device tokens from Apple and Google to deliver local notifications.

1.3 Information We Do Not Collect

HandyBot does not collect precise location data, contacts, calendar entries, or other files from your device beyond the photos you explicitly select to upload. We do not use advertising identifiers or sell personal data to advertisers.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve the Service, including generating repair plans, inventory analyses, and chat responses;
  • Process and deliver AI-generated content via third-party AI providers (see Section 4);
  • Send push notifications for maintenance reminders you have enabled;
  • Process subscription payments and manage your subscription status;
  • Respond to your inquiries and provide customer support;
  • Detect, investigate, and prevent fraudulent, unauthorized, or illegal activity;
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms of Service.

3. Local-First Data Storage

HandyBot is designed to be local-first. The following data is stored only on your device and is not transmitted to our servers:

  • Your repair history and repair plans;
  • Your home inventory items;
  • Your reminders and maintenance schedules;
  • Your chat conversations with HandyBot;
  • Photos you upload (stored in the app's document directory on your device).

If you delete the app or use the "Delete everything" feature in Settings, all locally-stored data is permanently removed. We cannot recover this data.

4. Third-Party Services and AI Processing

To provide AI-powered features, we send certain data to third-party service providers:

4.1 Anthropic (Claude AI)

When you request an AI-generated response (photo diagnosis, chat, inventory analysis, verification), we transmit the following to Anthropic, PBC for processing:

  • The photos or images you submit;
  • The text of your messages or prompts;
  • Contextual information about your repair (e.g., prior messages in the same conversation).

Anthropic processes this data under their own privacy policy, available at anthropic.com/legal/privacy. Per Anthropic's commercial terms, API inputs are not used to train their models by default.

4.2 Apple App Store and Google Play Store

Subscription billing, app distribution, and push notification delivery are handled by Apple Inc. and Google LLC under their respective privacy policies.

4.3 Future Service Providers

As HandyBot grows, we may introduce additional service providers for authentication, analytics, backend hosting, or customer support. We will update this policy when material changes occur.

5. Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only in the following circumstances:

  • Service Providers. With vendors who help us operate the Service (e.g., Anthropic, Apple, Google), under contracts that restrict their use of your data to providing services to us.
  • Legal Compliance. When required by law, subpoena, court order, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of grnd-lab, our users, or others.
  • Business Transfers. In connection with a merger, acquisition, financing, or sale of assets, subject to standard confidentiality protections.
  • With Your Consent. For any other purpose disclosed to you at the time of collection.

6. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the following rights:

  • Access. Request a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
  • Correction. Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Deletion. Request deletion of your personal information. You can delete all local data at any time via Settings → Delete Everything.
  • Portability. Request your data in a portable format.
  • Opt-Out. Disable push notifications in your device settings; disable AI features by not using them.

To exercise these rights, email us at support@grnd-lab.com. We will respond within 30 days.

6.1 California Residents (CCPA/CPRA)

If you are a California resident, you have additional rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act, including the right to know what personal information we collect, the right to delete, and the right to opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal information. We do not sell personal information.

6.2 European Economic Area, UK, and Switzerland (GDPR)

If you are in the EEA, UK, or Switzerland, our legal basis for processing your information is: (a) performance of our contract with you; (b) your consent; (c) our legitimate interests in providing and improving the Service; or (d) compliance with legal obligations. You have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

7. Data Security

We use commercially reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to protect your information. Data transmitted to third-party AI providers is sent over encrypted HTTPS connections. However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Important: As noted in our README, the current MVP bundles an Anthropic API key in the client. Production versions will route API calls through a secure backend proxy.

8. Children's Privacy

HandyBot is not intended for users under the age of 13 (or 16 in the EEA). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

9. Data Retention

Local data persists on your device until you delete it. Server-side data (e.g., support email correspondence) is retained for as long as necessary to provide the Service and comply with legal obligations, typically no more than 3 years after your last interaction unless a longer period is required by law.

10. International Data Transfers

Our service providers (including Anthropic) may be located in the United States or other countries. By using the Service, you consent to the transfer of your information to countries that may have different data protection laws than your country of residence.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will notify you through the app or by email at least 30 days before the changes take effect. Your continued use of the Service after the effective date constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Legal

Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 12, 2026 · Effective date: April 12, 2026

1. Introduction

GRND Labs LLC ("we," "our," or "us"), doing business as HandyBot, operates the HandyBot mobile application (the "App") and the website at handybot-app.com (the "Website"). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, the legal bases for processing, and your rights regarding your data. It applies to all users of the App and Website worldwide.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide

  • Account information: email address (optional), locally-generated user ID, display name.
  • Home profiles: home name, optional address, icon, and other home metadata you add.
  • Repair data: photos of items that need repair, text notes and messages in repair chats, repair plans (including tools, materials, steps, and completion status), difficulty and time estimates, and estimated savings.
  • Home inventory: photos of appliances, systems, and items; AI-generated condition assessments; age and lifespan estimates; notes and maintenance tips.
  • Maintenance reminders: reminder titles, frequencies, next-due dates, and completion history.
  • Chat content: photos and text sent in the Ask HandyBot conversational feature.
  • Support communications: when you email support@grnd-lab.com, we retain the content of your message and any attachments.

2.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • Device type, operating system version, and app version.
  • Anonymous usage analytics (screens viewed, features used) — only if you consent on first launch.
  • Crash reports and performance diagnostics.
  • Push notification tokens, when you enable reminders, to deliver local notifications via Apple and Google.

2.3 Information from Repair & Inventory Photos

When you use the AI repair diagnosis or home inventory features, your photo is sent to Anthropic's servers for analysis by Claude AI. Photos are processed to identify items, assess conditions, and generate repair plans. Anthropic does not retain photos after analysis completes and does not use API inputs to train its models. If you save a repair or inventory item, the photo is stored locally on your device (not on our servers).

2.4 Information We Do Not Collect

HandyBot does not collect precise location data, contacts, calendar entries, or files beyond the photos you explicitly choose to upload. We do not sell personal data to advertisers.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and improve the App's core features, including AI repair diagnosis, home inventory analysis, maintenance reminders, and step-by-step repair walkthroughs.
  • To analyze repair and inventory photos using Claude AI and generate repair plans, condition assessments, and maintenance schedules.
  • To send you local push notifications (e.g., maintenance reminders, scheduled tasks) if you opt in.
  • To process and verify subscription entitlements through the Apple App Store and Google Play Store.
  • To diagnose bugs and improve app performance.
  • To respond to your inquiries and provide customer support.
  • To comply with legal obligations and respond to lawful requests.

4. AI Processing & Automated Decision-Making

The App uses artificial intelligence (Anthropic's Claude Haiku and Sonnet models) to analyze repair photos, identify items in your home inventory, estimate conditions and lifespans, generate step-by-step repair plans, and respond to chat questions. This processing is automated but does not produce legal or similarly significant effects — it generates repair guidance, inventory assessments, and reminders that you can review, edit, and override before acting on them.

You always have the option to manually correct AI outputs, reject suggestions, or not act on any recommendation. AI-generated repair plans, condition assessments, and safety guidance are provided for informational and convenience purposes only and should not be relied upon for safety-critical decisions without independent verification from a qualified licensed professional (see our Terms of Service for full disclaimers).

5. Local-First Data Storage

HandyBot is designed to be local-first. The following data is stored only on your device and is not transmitted to our servers:

  • Your repair history and repair plans;
  • Your home inventory items;
  • Your reminders and maintenance schedules;
  • Your chat conversations with HandyBot;
  • Photos you upload (stored in the app's document directory on your device).

If you delete the app or use the "Delete everything" feature in Settings, all locally-stored data is permanently removed. We cannot recover this data.

6. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services to operate the App. Each has its own privacy policy governing how it handles data:

  • Anthropic (Claude AI): All AI repair photo analysis, home inventory analysis, chat responses, and repair verification are handled exclusively by Anthropic's Claude models (Haiku and Sonnet). Photos and text are sent to Anthropic's API for analysis. Anthropic does not use API inputs to train its models. No other third-party AI services are used. Anthropic Privacy Policy.
  • Apple App Store: If you downloaded the App from the Apple App Store, app distribution, in-app purchases, and subscriptions are handled by Apple Inc. under the Apple Privacy Policy.
  • Google Play Store: If you downloaded the App from the Google Play Store, app distribution, in-app purchases, and subscriptions are handled by Google LLC under the Google Privacy Policy.

As HandyBot grows we may introduce additional providers (for example, for authentication, analytics, or backend proxying of AI requests). We will update this policy when material changes occur.

7. Subscriptions & In-App Purchases

The App is free to download and includes core features. The App also offers optional paid subscriptions and one-time in-app purchases to unlock premium features. All transactions are processed by Apple or Google through their respective stores; we do not collect or store your payment card details, banking information, or store account passwords.

7.1 Premium Subscription ("HandyBot Pro")

The App offers an optional auto-renewable subscription that unlocks premium features (for example, unlimited AI repair diagnoses, expanded home inventory, advanced analytics, and priority support). Subscriptions renew automatically unless canceled at least 24 hours before the end of the current billing period. You can manage or cancel your subscription at any time in your Apple ID or Google Play account settings.

7.2 Information We Collect Related to Purchases

When you make a purchase, Apple or Google provides us with a transaction receipt that includes the transaction identifier, product purchased, purchase date, and subscription status (active, expired, or canceled). We use this information solely to verify your entitlements and deliver the features you purchased. We do not receive your payment method details.

8. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information to third parties. We share data only in the following circumstances:

  • With the third-party services listed in Section 6, solely to provide App functionality.
  • If required by law, regulation, subpoena, court order, or other valid legal process.
  • In connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of all or a portion of our assets, in which case you will be notified via the App or email.
  • With your consent, for any other purpose disclosed at the time of collection.

9. Data Retention

Because HandyBot is local-first, most of your data lives on your device until you delete it. Specific retention periods by category:

  • On-device data (homes, repairs, inventory, reminders, chat history, photos): retained on your device until you delete the item, use the in-app "Delete account & all data" flow, or uninstall the App.
  • Photos sent for AI analysis: transmitted to Anthropic and not retained by Anthropic after processing. Saved photos remain on your device only.
  • Subscription & purchase data: transaction receipts and entitlement records are retained for the duration of your account or as required for financial record-keeping and legal compliance.
  • Support communications: retained for up to 3 years after your last interaction unless a longer period is required by law.
  • Analytics data: anonymous, aggregated analytics (if you consent) may be retained indefinitely.

If you delete your account via the in-app flow or by emailing us, we will delete any server-held personal data within 30 days, except where we are required to retain it for legal or compliance purposes.

10. Your Rights and Choices

You have meaningful control over your data in HandyBot. Because HandyBot is local-first, many of these rights can be exercised directly within the app itself — no waiting for us to respond.

10.1 Right to Access and Portability (Export Your Data)

You can export a complete copy of all your data at any time:

  • In-app: Go to Settings → Export My Data. HandyBot generates a JSON file containing all of your homes, repairs, chat messages, inventory items, reminders, and settings. You can share or save this file anywhere.
  • By request: If you prefer, email support@grnd-lab.com with the subject line "Data Export Request" and we will provide your data within 30 days.

10.2 Right to Correction (Edit Your Data)

You can review and correct any data stored in the app directly:

  • Homes: Tap a home in the Homes screen to rename it, change its address, or update its icon.
  • Inventory items: Tap any item in Home Inventory to edit its name, category, condition, or notes.
  • Reminders: Tap any reminder to change its title, frequency, or next-due date.
  • Repairs: Open a repair to edit its title, notes, or step completion status.
  • By request: For anything you cannot edit in the app (e.g., correspondence with support), email support@grnd-lab.com.

10.3 Right to Deletion (Erase Your Data and Account)

You control when and how your data is erased:

  • Delete specific items: Long-press any repair, inventory item, reminder, or home to delete it individually.
  • Delete all data and account: Go to Settings → Delete Account & All Data. This permanently and irreversibly erases every home, repair, inventory item, reminder, chat message, photo, and your account. We cannot recover this data after deletion.
  • Uninstall: Deleting the app from your device also removes all locally-stored data.
  • By request: Email support@grnd-lab.com with the subject line "Deletion Request." We will confirm deletion within 30 days and delete any server-held records (such as support correspondence) except where we are legally required to retain them.

10.4 Other Rights

  • Object / Restrict Processing. You can disable push notifications in your device settings, stop using AI features (no data will be sent to Anthropic), or cancel your subscription at any time.
  • Withdraw Consent. Where we rely on your consent to process data, you can withdraw it at any time by deleting your data, uninstalling the app, or emailing us.
  • Non-Discrimination. We will not deny service, charge different prices, or provide a different quality of service because you exercised any of these rights.
  • Complaint. If you believe we have violated your privacy rights, you may lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

To exercise any right that is not available directly in the app, email us at support@grnd-lab.com. We will respond within 30 days. For identity verification, we may ask you to confirm the email address associated with your account.

11. Data Security

We use industry-standard security measures to protect your data, including encryption in transit (TLS 1.2+) between the App and our service providers. Photos and text sent to Anthropic's API are transmitted over encrypted HTTPS connections. On-device data is protected by your device's operating-system sandbox. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

12. Data Breach Notification

In the event of a data breach that poses a risk to your rights and freedoms, we will notify affected users via email and/or in-App notification without undue delay, and no later than required by applicable law (72 hours under GDPR, or as required by state law). We will also notify relevant supervisory authorities as required.

13. Cookies, Advertising & Tracking

13.1 Cookies on the Website

When you visit our Website we use cookies and similar technologies (including browser localStorage) to operate the site and — only with your consent — to measure usage, remember preferences, and support marketing activities. On your first visit you will see a cookie banner allowing you to accept all, reject all non-essential cookies, or customize your choices by category. You can change or withdraw your consent at any time by clicking "Cookie settings" in the website footer.

We classify cookies and similar technologies into four categories:

  • Strictly necessary: Required for the Website to function. This includes a single entry in your browser's local storage that records your own cookie-consent choices so we do not show you the banner on every page. These are active by default and do not require consent because the site could not operate without them.
  • Preferences: Remember non-essential choices you make on the site, such as UI preferences. Opt-in only.
  • Analytics: Help us understand how visitors use the site (for example, which pages are viewed and which buttons are clicked) so we can improve it. Opt-in only.
  • Marketing: Used to measure advertising effectiveness and, where applicable, to show relevant ads on other sites. Opt-in only.

Current status: As of the effective date of this policy, the Website does not set any Preferences, Analytics, or Marketing cookies. The only item we store is the strictly necessary record of your consent choice. If we add analytics or marketing technologies in the future, they will be gated behind your consent and disclosed in an updated version of this policy.

Legal basis (EU/UK/EEA). Where the GDPR, UK GDPR, and ePrivacy rules apply, we rely on your consent as the legal basis for setting any non-essential cookies. Strictly necessary cookies are set on the basis of our legitimate interest in operating the site. You may withdraw consent at any time via the Cookie settings link; withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal.

Browser-level controls. In addition to our in-site controls, most browsers let you block or delete cookies and site data through their settings. Blocking strictly necessary cookies may cause parts of the site to stop working.

13.2 In-App Tracking (Mobile App)

The App itself does not currently use cookies or third-party advertising. If we introduce in-app advertising in the future, we will gate any personalized ad tracking behind your consent via the App Tracking Transparency framework on iOS and an equivalent opt-in on Android, and we will update this policy.

13.3 Do Not Track Signals

Some browsers transmit "Do Not Track" (DNT) signals. There is currently no industry standard for how to respond to DNT signals. Our Website does not currently respond to DNT signals, but you can manage tracking preferences through the Cookie settings link described above or through your browser.

  • Right to Delete: You may request deletion of your personal information, subject to certain legal exceptions.
  • Right to Correct: You may request correction of inaccurate personal information.

Virginia, Colorado, Connecticut, Texas, Oregon & Other U.S. States

If you reside in a state with a comprehensive privacy law (such as the Virginia Consumer Data Protection Act, Colorado Privacy Act, Connecticut Data Privacy Act, Texas Data Privacy and Security Act, or Oregon Consumer Privacy Act), you generally have the right to:

  • Access, correct, and delete your personal data.
  • Obtain a portable copy of your data.
  • Opt out of targeted advertising, the sale of personal data (we do not sell your data), and profiling in furtherance of decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects.

  • Right to Object: You may object to processing based on legitimate interests.
  • Automated Decision-Making: The App uses AI to generate repair plans and condition estimates. This does not constitute automated decision-making with legal or similarly significant effects under Article 22 of the GDPR, as you can review, edit, and override all AI outputs.
  • Withdrawal of Consent: Where processing is based on your consent, you have the right to withdraw consent at any time without affecting the lawfulness of processing performed before withdrawal.
  • Supervisory Authority: You have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at support@grnd-lab.com. We will respond within the timeframe required by applicable law.

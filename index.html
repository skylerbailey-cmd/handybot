Recall Alerts

Know the Moment Your Stuff Gets Recalled

HandyBot checks the U.S. Consumer Product Safety Commission database every day and matches new recalls against the items in your home inventory by make and model. The instant your fridge, crib, or coffee maker is recalled, you get a push notification with the hazard, the remedy, and a link to the manufacturer.

  • ✓ Daily automated CPSC recall checks
  • ✓ Make- and model-level inventory matching
  • ✓ Push notifications the moment a match is found
  • ✓ Track each recall through New, Contacted, In Progress, Resolved
  • ✓ Hazard and remedy details surfaced inline

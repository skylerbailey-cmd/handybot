How It Works

From broken to fixed in four steps.

1

Snap a Photo

Take a photo of what's broken or needs fixing. Add an optional note to describe the issue.

2

AI Creates Your Plan

Claude AI analyzes the photo and generates a full repair plan — tools, materials, costs, steps, and safety notes.

3

Follow the Steps

Work through the interactive checklist. Chat with HandyBot if you get stuck or need help on a specific step.

4

Verify & Done

Upload a photo of the finished work. HandyBot verifies the quality and tracks your savings.

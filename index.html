AI Repair Verification

Upload a photo of your finished work. HandyBot's AI verifies the repair quality and confirms it's done right — or suggests fix-up steps before you close it out.

Warranty Tracker

Snap a photo of your receipt. HandyBot stores the purchase date, warranty provider, expiration, and coverage notes — and alerts you before warranties expire.

🚨

CPSC Recall Alerts

HandyBot checks the Consumer Product Safety Commission daily and matches recalls against your inventory by make and model. You get a push notification the moment your fridge, crib, or coffee maker is recalled.

💳

Insurance Export

One-tap PDF of your Schedule of Personal Property — every item, photo, make/model, serial number, and estimated replacement value. Send it to your insurance agent before disaster strikes.

👪

Family Sharing

Invite up to four household members. Everyone sees the same homes, repairs, and reminders — and an activity log shows who fixed what and when.

🧑‍🍻

Kids-Safe Mode

Let kids ask HandyBot questions safely. Dangerous topics (live electrical, gas, ladders) are blocked, and parents get a notification to review anything flagged.

🏢

Landlord Tools

Built for rental owners: unlimited homes, a vendor contact book, PDF invoice generator with labor and materials, and Schedule E expense categorization for tax season.

☁

Cloud Sync & Multi-Device

Sign in and your repairs, inventory, reminders, and photos sync securely across every device. Export or import a full home to move data to a new phone.

⚠

Safety-First Risk Flags

Every plan is screened for eight hazard categories — electrical, gas, plumbing, structural, height, chemical, fire, sharps. When work crosses the line, HandyBot tells you to call a pro.
